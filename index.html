    Chicken Qorma Recipe:

    Total Time: 1.5 to 2 hour

    Prep Time: 20 mins

    Cook Time: 1 – 1.5 hours

    For the Chicken Marinade:

    Ingredients:

    1. 1 kg chicken (bone-in, cut into medium pieces)

    2. ½ cup plain yogurt whisked until smooth

    3. 2 medium onions (thinly sliced for frying)

    4. ¼ cup cooking oil or ghee (for frying and cooking)

    5. 1 tbsp ginger paste

    6. 1 tbsp garlic paste

    7. Salt to taste (start with 1.5 tsp)

    8. ¼ cup water (adjust as needed)

      Whole Spices for aroma:

    • 2–3 green cardamoms

    • 1 black cardamom

    • 4–5 cloves

    • 1 bay leaf

    • ½ tsp black cumin seeds (shahi jeera, or regular cumin as substitute)

    • 5–6 black peppercorns

    Ground Sspice

    1. 1½ tsp coriander powder
    2. 1 tsp red chili powder (adjust to taste)
    3. ¼ tsp turmeric powder
    4. ½ tsp garam masala powder (added at the end)

      5. Optional Ingredients:

    5. 2 tbsp kewra water or rose water (for aroma)
    6. 2 tbsp ground almond or cashew paste (for richer, Mughlai-style qorma)

      7. Cooking Method

    7. Step 1 :Step 1: Fry the Onions (Birista)
    8. Heat ¼ cup oil or ghee in a heavy pot.
    9. Add thinly sliced onions and fry on medium heat until golden brown and crispy.
    10. Remove from oil, drain on a paper towel.
    11. Let cool, then lightly crush by hand or in a chopper.
    12. Tip:Don’t burn the onions — bitterness will ruin the flavor.
    1. Step 2:Sauté Whole Spices.
    2. In the same oil, add all whole spices.
    3. Sauté for 30 seconds until fragrant.
    4. Step 3:Add Chicken
    5. Add chicken pieces and fry on medium-high heat for 6–8 minutes until sealed and lightly golden.
    6. Add ginger and garlic paste, cook for 2–3 minutes until raw smell disappears.
    7. Step 4:Add Ground Spices and Yogurt
    8. Add red chili, turmeric, and coriander powder. Mix well.
    9. Lower the heat and add whisked yogurt slowly, stirring constantly to avoid curdling.
    10. Cook for 5–7 minutes until oil separates from the masala.
    11. Step 5:Add Fried Onions
    12. Add the crushed fried onions (birista) to the pot.
    13. Cook for another 5–10 minutes, stirring occasionally. The onions will melt into the curry, thickening and enriching the flavor.
    14. Step 6: Simmer and Finish
    15. Add ¼–½ cup water depending on your preferred gravy thickness.
    16. Cover and simmer on low heat for 15–20 minutes, or until chicken is fully cooked and tender.
    17. Add garam masala and optional kewra/rose water in the last 2 minutes.
    18. Optional Step:Creamier, Mughlai Qorma>
    19. Add 2 tbsp almond or cashew paste with the yogurt in Step 4 for a richer, royal-style Qorma.

    🫓 Serve With:

    • Naan, roti, or paratha
    • Plain basmati rice or jeera rice
    • Onion salad and lemon wedges

    Tips for Perfect Qorma

    1. Golden fried onions are the soul of the dish — don’t rush them.
    2. bone-in chicken for a deeper, more authentic flavor.
    3. Ghee gives a richer aroma — use in place of oil for authenticity.
